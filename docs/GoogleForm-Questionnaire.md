## Form Title
**CommunityPulse Survey – AAUA Students & Host Community Issues**

## Form Description
This short survey supports the design of **CommunityPulse**, a lightweight platform that allows AAUA students and host-community indigenes (Akungba, Iwaro, Etioro, Ayegunle and surrounding areas) to report and track common issues.

Your answers are anonymous and will help shape the categories, features and priority areas of the system.  
Estimated time: 4–6 minutes. Thank you.

## How to Create the Google Form

1. Go to [https://forms.google.com](https://forms.google.com) → **Blank form**.  
2. Set the title and description exactly as above.  
3. Create three sections matching the headings.  
4. Use the correct question types (Multiple choice, Checkboxes, Linear scale, Short answer, Paragraph).  
5. Mark questions 1, 5, 7, 8, 9 as required.  
6. (Optional) Turn off “Collect email addresses” for full anonymity.  
7. Click **Send → Link → Shorten** and copy the link.  
8. Share via WhatsApp groups (SUG, departmental, community associations), Facebook pages, and class platforms.  
9. After responses are collected, download as CSV/Excel for analysis.
